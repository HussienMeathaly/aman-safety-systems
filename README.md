# Remix of Mir'at Al-Aman (Mirror of Security)

Prompt:

I will provide you with a website URL. I want you to fully analyze and understand the entire project in depth, including:

All pages and their structure

Layout, sections, and components

User experience and navigation flow

Content hierarchy and design patterns

Typography (fonts), spacing, and styling

Then, based on this analysis:

Recreate a fully similar website in structure, layout, and experience.

Adapt the design to match my company’s branding, which I will provide (logo + brand identity).

Keep the same font style (or the closest possible match if exact font is unavailable).

Maintain the same layout and section flow, but:

Replace all images (especially on the homepage) with new ones relevant to safety and security systems.

Ensure the new visuals match the same style, composition, and placement.

Rewrite content where needed to align with my company’s services and industry (safety & security) while preserving the same tone and structure.

Arabic Requirements (VERY IMPORTANT):

The entire website must be in Arabic language only.

Use RTL (Right-to-Left) layout across all pages.

Ensure proper Arabic typography (no broken letters or spacing issues).

Use professional Arabic-friendly fonts (similar style to the original if possible).

All UI elements, menus, buttons, and forms must be in Arabic.

Ensure the final result is:

Modern and clean

Fully responsive (mobile, tablet, desktop)

Optimized for performance and usability

Important:

Do NOT copy content literally; adapt it.

Preserve the design logic and user journey.

Keep consistency across all pages.

I will provide:

Website URL: https://dubai.com.sa/

Logo and brand identity files

Your goal is to deliver a high-quality Arabic website replica adapted to my business identity, not just a visual copy.
